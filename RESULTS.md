## Metric definitions

- **AP:** detection average precision, averaged over IoU thresholds 0.50 to 0.95 in increments of 0.05, across the dataset categories and the evaluator's all-area range, with maxDets=1500.
- **AP50 / AP75:** corresponding detection AP at IoU 0.50 / 0.75.
- **APvt / APtiny / APsmall / APmedium:** AP over the evaluator's verytiny, tiny, small, and medium area ranges. The nominal area ranges in pixel squared are `[0,64]`, `[64,256]`, `[256,1024]`, and `[1024,10000000000]`. Boundary handling follows the AI-TOD evaluator implementation; these labels must not be replaced by ordinary COCO area bins.
- **AR1500:** detection average recall with evaluator maxDets=1500, averaged over the same IoU thresholds and the all-area range.
- **ARvt:** corresponding verytiny-area detection average recall with maxDets=1500.

These are final detection metrics, not encoder-candidate coverage, center-response hit rates, or initial-reference IoU. A difference between two percentage values is expressed in **percentage points**, not a relative percentage gain.

## Reported comparison protocol

The manuscript reports AI-TOD-V2 **trainval → test**, ResNet-50, and a shared **24-epoch** training protocol for this comparison system. The stated reference recipe uses microbatch size 1, accumulation 3, AMP, main/backbone learning rates 1e-4/1e-5, MultiStepLR milestones 13 and 23, and weight decay 1e-4. See [reproduction guidance](docs/REPRODUCIBILITY.md).

The final experiments use the same complete AI-TOD-V2 benchmark and split as the original DQ-DETR paper: **14,018 trainval images for training and 14,018 test images for evaluation**. The original paper describes 28,036 images and 752,745 annotated instances across the entire dataset, with 11,214 train and 2,804 validation images. These are the original paper's benchmark statistics, not newly counted release artifacts. Per-split annotation counts and final annotation-file checksums are not supplied here. See [DQ-DETR Section 4.1 and Table 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.03507v3#S4.SS1).

The four add-on configurations correspond respectively to `baseline`, `aux_only`, `aux_lqs`, and `full`. They configure only this module package, not the full training environment. The paper describes the last two methods as differing by SARI; this release has not independently verified their original per-run assets or reconstructed their full training state. Do not interpret the table as proof that any arbitrary upstream checkout, initialization, or seed will reproduce the same scores.

The comparison baseline here is the paper's selected reproduced baseline, **29.2328 AP**, not the original publication's external baseline number and not a different historical local baseline.
